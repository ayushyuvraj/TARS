QUICK START CHEAT SHEET (3 SIMPLE STEPS):

1. CHECK PREREQUISITES:
   Double-click:  verify_environment.bat
   Confirm Python 3.11+ (64-bit) and Node.js 20+ LTS are installed.

2. RUN SETUP (ONE TIME):
   Double-click:  setup.bat
   This automatically creates your .venv and installs all dependencies.

3. LAUNCH TARS:
   Double-click:  run.bat  (or START_TARS.bat)
   The app will automatically start and open in your browser at:
   http://127.0.0.1:5173
